APP PRIVACY POLICY

Dots and Boxes
Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Summary: Dots and Boxes is an entertainment game. It has no accounts, sign-up or login, and we do not collect your name, email or other personal details. The game works offline, and the computer opponent runs entirely on your device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect certain device information to deliver and measure those ads, as described below.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Mavericks App ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the mobile game Dots and Boxes (the "App"), available on Google Play and, where released, the Apple App Store. By using the App you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please uninstall the App.

2. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask you to provide personal information. We operate no servers that receive your personal data from the App, so we do not collect, store or have access to your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files or payment details.

The App may store game progress and settings, such as board size, player names you type for local games, scores, difficulty level and sound preferences, locally on your device so the game works as expected. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Games against the computer opponent and local multiplayer games are played entirely on your device.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a third-party service. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the App may automatically collect and use information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA);
  • IP address and the approximate location that can be derived from it;
  • device and software details such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • ad interactions and in-app activity related to ads, such as views, taps and performance data;
  • diagnostic and security data used to detect fraud and keep the ad service working.

This information is collected and processed by Google under its own policies, not by us. We do not receive your advertising identifier or ad profile. Depending on your region and settings, you may see personalized or non-personalized ads. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. How information is used

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, we do not use it for profiling, marketing or sale. Locally stored game data is used only to run the game on your device. Information processed by Google AdMob is used by Google to display, measure and secure advertising.

5. Third-party services

The only third-party service we have integrated is Google AdMob. Google Play and the Apple App Store, which distribute the App, also handle information under their own privacy policies when you download or update it. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.

6. Analytics

We do not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools in the App. The advertising SDK may collect technical and diagnostic data as described in section 3.

7. Your choices and controls

  • Android: open Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads) to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization.
  • iPhone and iPad: open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking to control whether apps may ask to track you, and Apple Advertising settings for personalized ads.
  • Google: manage ad personalization at Google My Ad Center.
  • Consent: where required, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the App shows a consent message before personalized ads are served. You can change your choice from the privacy options in the App where available.

Opting out of personalized ads does not remove ads; you may still see ads that are less relevant to you.

8. Data storage and international transfers

Game data stays on your device. Information handled by Google AdMob may be processed on Google servers in countries other than your own, in line with Google's policies and applicable law.

9. Data retention and deletion

We do not keep personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. Locally stored game data is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage in your device settings. Data held by Google for advertising is retained under Google's policies, and you can manage or delete it through the links in section 7. If you believe we hold information about you, email us and we will respond promptly.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, or laws in certain US states such as California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data, to object to certain processing, and to opt out of targeted advertising. As we hold no personal data about you, we generally cannot identify you in our records. For data processed by Google AdMob, please use Google's tools above or contact Google directly. We do not sell personal information. You can contact us at any time with questions or requests.

11. Children's privacy

Dots and Boxes is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA, where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us. Parents may also use their device's parental controls and ad settings.

12. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the App and avoid collecting data we do not need; the best protection is that we collect no personal data ourselves. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your device's operating system and the App up to date.

13. Links to other sites

The App and this page may link to third-party sites such as those of Google. We do not control them and recommend reading their privacy policies.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add features or services (such as online multiplayer). The latest version will always be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Please check back from time to time.

15. Contact us

Questions or requests about this policy? Email mobileapps.ajinkya@gmail.com. This policy is published at mavericksapp.github.io/privacy/dots-and-boxes.html.
