Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Important: This is the general privacy policy for the Mavericks App website. Each app has its own privacy policy, which takes priority for that app.

1. Introduction

Mavericks App ("we", "us", "our") develops and publishes mobile applications and games. We respect your privacy and aim to be clear about how information is handled.

2. Information collected by this website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use forms, user accounts, advertising or analytics on this site, and we do not intentionally collect personal information through it.

As the host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address and browser details when pages are requested. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you choose a light or dark theme, that choice is saved only in your browser (local storage) and is not sent to us.

If you email us, we receive the information you include, such as your email address and message. We use it only to respond to you and provide support.

3. Our apps

Apps may use device permissions, local storage, advertising, or other services depending on the app. Please read the privacy policy of the specific app you use:

  • Connect Dots Privacy Policy
  • Dots and Boxes Privacy Policy

4. Third-party services

Some apps may use third-party services such as advertising providers or app-store services. Where an app uses them, its own privacy policy describes them.

5. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party providers when needed to operate a particular app or respond to a support request.

6. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by our services. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children. App-specific age requirements and child-privacy practices are stated in each app's privacy policy.

8. Your choices

If you contact us and want the information you provided removed, email us at the address below. Requests may be subject to reasonable verification and legal requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is published on this page with an updated date.
